FOUNDATION READINGOxfordAQA specification 3.5 · Textbook Chapter 8, pp. 226–229

A computer network connects computers or other devices through communication links so they can communicate and exchange data. Networks allow files, printers, software, messages, security services and backups to be shared. They also create costs and risks, including equipment and maintenance, unauthorised access, malware spreading and loss of shared resources after a network failure.

LAN and WAN describe geographical scale. Wired and wireless describe how data travels. A Wi-Fi network on one school site can therefore still be a LAN.
