Activity 2

Build a Simple 3D Table

Use basic shapes to build a small table. This teaches you how to resize shapes, move objects carefully, and check your model from different angles.

Must include

  • 1 large cuboid for the tabletop
  • 4 thin cuboids or cylinders for legs
  • At least 2 colours

Remember

  • Resize the shapes carefully
  • Lift the tabletop above the legs
  • Rotate your view to check the side

Good challenge

  • Add a small object on top
  • Keep the model neat
  • Do not use too many shapes yet
Please complete all checklist items and answer both questions.
