# Local dependencies

- Skulpt and Skulpt standard library, reused from the school's existing `year9-week2-project/vendor` folder. Copyright and licence notices remain in the bundled source. Skulpt supports Python in a browser; https://skulpt.org/ and https://github.com/skulpt/skulpt.
- jsPDF, reused from the same existing app. MIT licence and author notices remain in `libraries/jspdf.umd.min.js`; https://github.com/parallax/jsPDF.
- Raleway variable font, copied from the teacher's installed font. Raleway is distributed under the SIL Open Font License; https://github.com/theleagueof/raleway.
- School learning images were supplied by the teacher. They are not generic stock assets. Use within the school's authorised teaching context.

No dependency is fetched by the lesson at runtime.
