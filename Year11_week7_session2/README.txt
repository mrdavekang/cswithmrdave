YEAR 11 - TERM 1 - WEEK 7 - SESSION 2
Data Detectives: images, sound and compression

START HERE
Open index.html, not key-terms.html. The latter is the standalone key-term component.
This is a self-contained static app for GitHub Pages. No installation, subscription,
tracking service or database is required. All fonts, school images, scripts and
encrypted marking PDFs are included locally. For reliable secure teacher
verification, use the HTTPS GitHub Pages version.

LESSON ROUTE (60 minutes)
Read first 4; Do Now 6; types of learning 2; Main Task 1 18;
feedback/retry 3; Main Task 2 including marking 17; learning pit stop 3;
independent plenary 7. Extension is optional, not an extra required timed stage.
Evidence upload and scoreboard are supporting pages, not further teaching tasks.
Allow photographs after written work or collect papers if time is short.

PAPER FIRST
Collect printed key terms, core question paper and revision notebook.
Answers and corrections are written on paper. Only marks, learning selections,
completion checks and paper photographs are entered in the app.
Core: 16 published marks (images 4, sound 5, compression 7).
Optional extension: 11 published marks. Key-term recall: 10 classroom marks.
Independent plenary: 10 classroom marks. Do not combine these into a mock grade.
Official question papers are supplied separately to the teacher for printing;
they are deliberately NOT in this publicly hostable folder.

SAVE YOUR WORK
Student records and photographs are saved in this browser's IndexedDB, by name
and class. No work is sent to the teacher or another device automatically.
Private browsing, browser cleanup or moving to another browser can lose records.
Use Full backup regularly to download an editable JSON copy including photos.
Restore backup imports the copy, after confirmation. Use Save PDF for Teams.
Check that all handwriting is readable in the resulting PDF before submitting.
The app does not upload or submit anything to Microsoft Teams itself.
Shared devices: choose Change student; the next person must enter their own name.
Stored records are NOT protected against other users of the same browser/device.
Use individual school-managed student devices; do not collect confidential data.

TEACHER CONTROLS
The normal landing page contains no teacher-mode button.
Open index.html?teacher=1 and enter the separate PIN in the private teacher guide.
Teacher preview can inspect every stage. Preview responses are not saved.
The team leaderboard is entered and verified manually by the teacher.
Open the projector board from the teacher console on the SAME computer/browser.
Two rounds, 4 XP each: clear method 1, justified check 1, independent retry 2.
Recognition badges do not add XP. There is no speed reward or hint penalty.
Student devices do not synchronise points or response data to the teacher.
Board backup/restore is available in the teacher console.
Only team aliases and teacher-verified XP appear on the projected board.
Do not put pupil names, marks or RAG selections in the team aliases.

MARKING
The four marking PDFs are encrypted with a password supplied by the teacher.
Keep that password private until the first attempts of BOTH main tasks are done.
The teacher-console PIN is different from the PDF marking password.
The static PIN gate is a classroom deterrent, NOT secure server authentication.
The PDF password protects the PDF contents, rather than just hiding a web link.
Check school permission before distributing or publicly hosting protected extracts.
Keep private teacher notes, original papers and mock-use records outside this folder.
