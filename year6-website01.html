Plan Your Minecraft Biome Build

You will plan with your assigned partner. Choose one biome and design a structure that fits the climate and environment.

Plains

Open grassland, wide space, simple farms or villages.

Forest

Lots of trees, wood materials, cabins or hidden bases.

Savanna

Warm grassland, dry feeling, acacia-style builds.

Warm Ocean

Water, coral colours, islands or ocean research bases.

Cherry Grove

Pink trees, gentle landscape, peaceful garden builds.

Desert

Hot and dry, sand, cactus, sandstone towers or shelters.

Ice Plains

Cold, snowy, icy, research stations or snow shelters.

Planning rules

  1. Plan with your assigned partner only.
  2. Your structure must match the biome and climate.
  3. Use your plan when you start Minecraft later.
  4. Write enough detail so your website can explain your build clearly.
