# Year 9 — Your App, Your Choice

A 60-minute independent app-design lesson with English–Mandarin support and two Main Tasks.

## Student access

https://mrdavekang.github.io/cswithmrdave/year9-app-design-theory/

Teacher guidance: append `?teacher=1`.

## Included

- MIT school-email setup with genuine screenshot guides and a route to continue while waiting for email.
- Open-ended app topics, audience and purpose.
- App investigation, labelled digital or paper sketch, two interactions and one improvement.
- WAGBA, knowledge, skills and understanding; Types of Learning and Learning Pit Stop.
- Browser autosave, PDF printing, readable report and editable backup.
- Universal extra prompts, touch and keyboard sketch controls, and paper/photo alternative.

Work is stored in this browser. Save a PDF and backup before changing device. MIT credentials are entered only on MIT's own website. The main submission is the PDF uploaded in Teams as directed by the teacher.

No installation or build step is needed. Keep all files together. The first lesson designs an app; implementation in MIT follows in the practical lesson.
