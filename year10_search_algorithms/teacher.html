Lesson preparation and resources

Open the school-template projector, select Enable overview sound there once, then select Start 3-min setup + overview here. After three minutes, the 3:13 opening film plays. When the projector confirms the film has ended, the four-minute Starter starts automatically. Pause and Resume also control the film. If sound is blocked, use the clear play button on the projector; the class timer waits. Other stages cancel the opening sequence. The original nine stage timers plus this film total about 58:14, leaving about 1:46 flexibility in a 60-minute lesson. Students still study the two full assigned YouTube videos in Main 1 and Main 2; no mid-lesson film auto-plays.

Full video playback takes roughly 16 minutes, so use the flexible timers and shorter core traces. Saved drafts are not evidence of attention.
