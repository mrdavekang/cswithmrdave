Stage timer

05:00

  1. 00–05 · Mission. Play the model. Ask: “What moved? What stayed still?”
  2. 05–11 · Do Now and Types of Learning. Point to Face, Frame 2 and the sheet. Ask for an example of knowledge, skill and understanding.
  3. 11–31 · Main Task 1. Show import once, then duplicate Calm and change just two eyes. Give absent students Garden Guide or the blank canvas. Check 3 distinct frames; 4–5 are a stretch.
  4. 31–48 · Main Task 2. Demonstrate one sprite with three costumes, then green flag → first costume → forever with wait and next costume. Use Python only on devices with Tkinter; edit delay or frame order, not the window code.
  5. 48–55 · Pit Stop and Go Further. Partners predict, test and compare timing. Fast finishers make Surprise hold for longer.
  6. 55–60 · Plenary and submission. Collect native sheet, evidence note and .sb3 or .py. Review quiz is follow-up practice: 30 correct.

What to look for

  • Frame count: at least three distinguishable expressions, one aligned character.
  • Technical model: one sheet and individual frames; native dimensions are (16 × frame count) by 16.
  • Programming: deterministic start and visible loop, with a deliberate delay.
  • Reasoning: one observation comparing before and after a timing/order change.
