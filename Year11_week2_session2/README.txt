YEAR 11 COMPUTER SCIENCE — WEEK 2 SESSION 2
Choosing the Right Network

HOW TO USE
1. Keep all files and folders together.
2. Open index.html in a browser, or upload the folder contents to a GitHub Pages repository.
3. Students enter their name and class. Work autosaves in that browser.
4. At the end, students download their PDF learning report and submit it to Teams.

LESSON CONTENT
- Starter: retrieve the network definition and infer increasing geographical scale.
- Main Activity 1: distinguish LAN/WAN from wired/wireless and correct common misconceptions.
- Main Activity 2: select and justify copper, fibre or wireless arrangements for an expanded school.
- Extension: original November 2025 Paper 2 Questions 07.1–07.3 shown as an unchanged page image.
- Plenary: three decisions covering classification, comparison and transmission-medium suitability.

SOURCES
- OxfordAQA International GCSE Computer Science textbook, “LANs and WANs” and “Types of network”, PDF pages 232–234.
- OxfordAQA International GCSE Computer Science specification, section 3.5.
- OxfordAQA International GCSE Computer Science 9210/2, November 2025, Questions 07.1–07.3.

NOTES
- The official mark scheme is not included in this student package.
- The full November 2025 question paper is in the resources folder.
- Student data remains in local browser storage unless the student exports the PDF.
- Clearing browser data may remove saved responses.
