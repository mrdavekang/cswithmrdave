TEACHER GUIDE
Year 11 · Term 1 · Week 3 · Session 1 · Network Topologies

WAGBA
Explaining and comparing star and bus topologies, then applying their trade-offs to a network scenario.

ASSESSMENT ALIGNMENT
This lesson primarily develops OxfordAQA AO1 and AO2.
- AO1: recall, explain and draw star and bus topologies.
- AO2: apply topology properties to faults, traffic, cost, expansion and scenarios.
OxfordAQA AO3 is predominantly concerned with analysing computational problems and developing/testing programmed solutions, so the scenario work is not labelled AO3.

SUGGESTED TIMING
- Overview and Do Now: 7 minutes
- Visual reading and checkpoint: 10 minutes
- Main Activity 1 comparison and diagrams: 15 minutes
- Main Activity 2 model and independent scenario: 17 minutes
- Extension: for early finishers
- Plenary and report export: 7 minutes

LEARNING-DESIGN DECISIONS
- Prior knowledge is retrieved before topology images are shown.
- Images are paired with concise adjacent explanations.
- Students reconstruct diagrams rather than only viewing them.
- One scenario is modelled through requirement, property, consequence, decision and trade-off.
- The second scenario fades the scaffold and requires independent explanation.
- Feedback is followed by a mark-reclaim or correction action.
- The plenary retrieves knowledge and does not repeat a confidence survey.

CONTENT ACCURACY
- Course textbook reference: printed pages 230–231.
- A terminator absorbs signals and prevents unwanted reflections; it does not turn a signal around and resend it.
- Physical bus networks have limited modern application. The exhibition is explicitly framed as a simplified examination comparison scenario.
