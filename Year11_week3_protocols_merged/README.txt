YEAR 11: PROTOCOLS AND TCP/IP
Term 1, Week 3, Sessions 2 and 3 combined

Open index.html. The app is static: no installation, server account or API key is needed.
For GitHub Pages, upload this entire folder, including assets, resources and vendor.
Use the existing repository's GitHub Pages settings. Do not upload the separate teacher folder.

Students enter a name and class. Use the same spelling when returning on the same device/browser.
Work saves locally; no work is sent to the teacher automatically.
Students download their evidence PDF, check it, and attach it to their Teams assignment.
The editable JSON backup can be restored through the final lesson stage on another device.
Downloaded backups and photographs contain student work: keep them private.
This is not a class dashboard or a secure student-account service. Use individual school devices.

Paper option: worksheet.html links to the A4 worksheet and blank card template.
Students may write on paper and upload photographs without typing the answers again.
Use JPG, PNG or WebP (six photographs per stage, maximum 12 MB each).
If a phone uses HEIC, export its photographs as JPG before uploading.

The 60-minute core lesson includes a 10-mark individual application task.
The original 13-mark extension is optional during class, or can be assigned as homework.
Written responses are not automatically graded. A completed stage is not a mastery grade.

Exam extract: June 2022 Paper 2 Questions 11–12; June 2024 Paper 2 Question 13.
Original wording, diagrams and marks are retained. The top of June 2024 p.24 is cropped

These sources retain their original copyright. Check your school's permissions before
publishing licensed textbook/examination material on an unrestricted public website.
No official mark scheme is included in this student folder.

RALeway and jsPDF are bundled to support offline use and PDF export.
No analytics, advertising or external student-data service is used.
