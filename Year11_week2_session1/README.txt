YEAR 11 COMPUTER SCIENCE — WEEK 2 SESSION 1
Computer Networks Interactive Lesson

HOW TO USE
1. Keep all files and folders together.
2. Open index.html in a browser, or upload the folder contents to a GitHub Pages repository.
3. Students enter their name and class. Work autosaves in that browser.
4. At the end, students download their PDF learning report and submit it to Teams.

LESSON CONTENT
- Starter: connected versus stand-alone computers, with embedded-systems retrieval.
- Main Activity 1: textbook reading, definition refinement, evidence classification and developed explanations.
- Main Activity 2: a contextual decision about networking a school examination office.
- Extension: original June 2025 Paper 2 Question 10.4 shown as an unchanged screenshot.
- Plenary: a definition, one developed benefit and one developed risk.

SOURCES
- OxfordAQA International GCSE Computer Science textbook, printed pages 226–227 (PDF pages 231–232).
- OxfordAQA International GCSE Computer Science specification, section 3.5, printed page 27.
- OxfordAQA International GCSE Computer Science 9210/2, June 2025, Question 10.4.

NOTES
- The official mark scheme is not included in this student package.
- The full June 2025 question paper is in the resources folder.
- All student data remains in local browser storage unless the student exports the PDF.
- Clearing browser data may remove saved responses.
